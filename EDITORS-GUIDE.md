# Writing for the Finbloom Learn page

A guide for everyone who writes, edits, or publishes articles. You don't need
any technical setup — everything happens in your browser.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to **<https://www.sanity.io/welcome>**
2. Sign in with the email you were invited on.
3. Click **Finbloom Capital**.

That's the editor. Bookmark it.

**Can't see Finbloom Capital after signing in?** You haven't been invited yet, or
you were invited on a different email address. Ask whoever manages the site to
add you — it takes them a minute.

> **A note on links.** You may have been sent a link to `sanity.io/manage`. That
> is the settings area, and there is nothing to write there. If you land on a
> page showing tokens, datasets or billing, you're in the wrong place — go back
> to sanity.io/welcome.

---

## How the editor is laid out

Down the left you'll see:

- **Learn Page Settings** — the headline and section titles on the Learn page
- **Articles** — the blog posts
- **Categories** — the filter buttons readers use
- **Authors** — writer names, photos and bios
- **Videos** — the "Watch & Learn" section
- **Resources** — the downloadable guides

Click any of them to see what's already there.

---

## Before your first article

Two things must already exist, because every article has to be attached to them:

- **a Category** — the topic, e.g. *Cash Flow*
- **an Author** — the person whose name goes on the piece

If either is missing, create it first. Both take about thirty seconds.

**To add an author:** *Authors → Create new*. Name is the only thing strictly
required, but do fill in:

- **Photo** — appears next to the byline
- **Short role** — sits under the name on the article, e.g. *Chief Risk Officer, Finbloom*
- **Bio** — one paragraph. **The author card at the bottom of an article only
  appears once this is filled in.** Leave it blank and readers see no bio at all.

**To add a category:** *Categories → Create new*. Give it a title. *Sort order*
decides where it sits in the row of filter buttons — lower numbers go first.

## Writing an article

Go to **Articles → Create new**, then work down the form.

### The Content tab

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The headline. |
| **Slug** | The article's web address. It fills in automatically from your title — you can usually leave it. |
| **Excerpt** | The short summary on preview cards and under the headline. Keep it under 320 characters; the editor will tell you if you go over. |
| **Main image** | The large photo. Drag one in, then **click the image and set the crop point** so it still looks right when cropped to a small card. Add **Alt text** describing the picture — screen readers rely on it. |
| **Category** | Pick one. |
| **Author** | Pick one. |
| **Body** | The article itself. |

### The Metadata tab

Click **Metadata** at the top of the form for these:

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Published at** | Defaults to right now. Set it to a future date to schedule the post. |
| **Read time** | Leave it blank — it's worked out from the length of your article. Only fill it in if you want to override that. |
| **Feature this article** | Promotes the article to the big card at the top of the Learn page. |

### Then publish

Press **Publish**, bottom right.

**It takes about a minute to appear on the website.** That's normal — don't
re-publish if you don't see it straight away. Refresh after a minute.

## Formatting your article

Use the style dropdown at the top of the Body box for:

- **Normal** — ordinary paragraphs
- **Section heading** — your main headings
- **Sub heading** — headings underneath those
- **Quote** — a quoted passage
- Bulleted and numbered lists, **bold**, *italic*, and links

Click the **+** button inside the Body box to add:

- **Image** — with a caption if you want one
- **Callout box** — the teal tip box. It has a small label at the top, which
  starts as "LAGOS SME TIP" but you can change to anything.
- **Pull quote** — the large italic quote that breaks up long sections

### The contents list writes itself

Articles show an **"In This Guide"** list down the side, which readers use to
jump around, and which follows them as they scroll.

You don't build it. It's made automatically from your **Section headings** and
**Sub headings**. Use headings and readers get a contents list; use none and
there simply isn't one. That's the only thing controlling it.

## Featuring and scheduling

**Featuring.** Tick *Feature this article* and it fills the large card at the top
of the Learn page, with a "FEATURED GUIDE" tag on the article itself.

Only one article is featured at a time — whichever featured article was
published most recently wins. So to swap the featured piece, just tick the new
one. **You don't need to untick the old one**, and the featured article never
shows up twice, because it's automatically left out of the grid below.

**Scheduling.** Set *Published at* to a date in the future and press Publish. The
article stays completely hidden until that moment, then appears on its own. You
don't need to do anything else, and nobody can reach it early.

---

## Changing an article after it's live

Open it, edit, press **Publish** again. The change is live within a minute.

**One thing to be careful with: the Slug.** That's the article's web address.
Changing it after publishing breaks every existing link to the piece — anything
shared on LinkedIn, sent in a newsletter, or saved by a reader stops working.
Fixing a typo in the *Title* is always safe; leave the *Slug* alone once it's
out.

**To take an article down:** open it and either set *Published at* to a future
date, or use the **⋮** menu next to the Publish button and choose *Unpublish*.

## Videos and resources

**Videos** — add a title, a thumbnail, and a YouTube or Vimeo link, and it plays
directly on the page. If you leave the link empty, the video shows as a still
image with no play button, which is a good way to tease something that isn't out
yet. One video can be ticked as featured to fill the large player.

**Resources** — these are the downloadable guides. Either upload a file *or*
paste an external link; if you do both, the link is used. With neither, the
button appears greyed out rather than broken. *Button label* controls the wording
on the button.

---

## Learn Page Settings

This holds the big headline at the top of the Learn page and the section titles
further down. There's only one of these — you edit it rather than creating new
ones.

Anything you clear out falls back to the original wording, so you can't
accidentally leave the page with a blank heading.

## If something looks wrong

| What you're seeing | What's happening |
| --- | --- |
| Published, but not on the site | Give it a minute. Then check *Published at* isn't set to a future date. |
| Article missing from the grid | If it's the featured one, it's in the big card at the top instead — it's deliberately not shown twice. |
| No author bio at the foot of the article | The author's **Bio** field is empty. |
| No "In This Guide" list | The article has no Section headings. |
| An older article has vanished from the Learn page | The page shows the 50 most recent articles. Older ones still work on their own web address, they're just not listed. |
| Can't find the project after signing in | You've not been invited, or you're signed in with a different email. |

Anything else, or if something looks broken rather than confusing, send a
screenshot to whoever manages the site.

---

## Worth knowing

- **There's no draft-only staging site.** What you publish is live. Use the
  scheduling field if you want to prepare something in advance.
- **Your work saves as you type.** Nothing is lost if you close the tab — but
  it isn't public until you press Publish.
- **Two people can edit the same article at once** and you'll see each other's
  changes as they happen.
